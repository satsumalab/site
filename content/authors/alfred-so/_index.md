Alfred So is a PhD student at University College of London (UCL) funded by the CRUK City of London fellowship. His fellowship focuses on investigating explainable radiological biomarkers of aggressiveness in stage I non-small cell lung cancers using conventional CT and phase-contrast micro-CT imaging along with digital pathology. This will be a collaborative effort across UCL and King's College of London.
Alfred graduated from The University of Manchester Medical School in 2018 and is a Medical Oncology Registrar at St Bartholomew's Hospital. In 2024, he was granted the Pan-London Cancer Alliance Fellowship to investigate the prognostic role of radiomics in stage II-III lung cancers with the Satsuma Lab.
